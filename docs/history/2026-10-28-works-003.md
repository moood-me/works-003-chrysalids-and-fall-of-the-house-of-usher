# works-003: The Chrysalids and The Fall of the House of Usher, gathered (2026-10-28)

The owner regroups the old worlds and games into numbered repositories `works-NNN-…`; this is the third: the two
worlds on engine 3 and their bespoke games — the ones that are played.

## What and how

- **Gathered with their histories**, each from its `env/dev` (`git filter-repo --to-subdirectory-filter`, then
  `git merge --allow-unrelated-histories` into `main`, one merge commit each): `moood-me/pavel--chrysalids` →
  `worlds/chrysalids/`, `pavel--fall-of-the-house-of-usher` → `worlds/fall-of-the-house-of-usher/`,
  `pavel--game--chrysalids` → `games/chrysalids/`, `pavel--game--fall-of-the-house-of-usher` →
  `games/fall-of-the-house-of-usher/`.
- **Not taken:** their earlier versions — v1 and v2 worlds (`khrizalidy`, `khrizalidy-kino`, `khrizalidy-kino-igra`,
  `khrizalidy-kino-igra-v2`, `khrizalidy-bespoke`, `padenie-doma-asherov`, `padenie-doma-asherov-v2`) and their games
  (`game--khrizalidy`, `game--khrizalidy-kino`, `game--padenie-doma-asherov`, `game--padenie-doma-asherov-v2`).
- **As moood has them now** (moood's `check` passes): worlds and games declare `"languages": ["en", "ru"]`; a game's
  `"world"` is the world's path (`../../worlds/<slug>`); `people.json` without `name` (a person is `{voice, how,
  short}`); `directions` removed from five scenes (moood dropped them); the meeting house's music at 60 bpm (Lyria's
  least; it asked for 56); the games' own `bespoke/check/run.mjs` names its report's printer `print`, not `say` (the
  language's own name); the old moood's `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` removed; translations laid out by `check --write`.
- **Docs**, the earlier versions' included: `docs/khrizalidy/`, `docs/usher/`, `docs/asherov/` from `pavel-zhur/rpg`;
  their history from `moood-me/studio` (`docs/history/`). What authors learn from them stays in moood
  (`docs/games/`, `docs/worlds/`). References to docs elsewhere are written `<owner>/<repo>:<path>`.
- The old repositories are left as they are.
