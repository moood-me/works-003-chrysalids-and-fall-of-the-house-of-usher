## Names

- **The Chrysalids** — «Die Chrysaliden». German editions appeared as «Wem gehört die Erde?» and later under other
  titles; «Die Chrysaliden» keeps the English title's image (the English source is followed here, not the Russian
  «Отклонение от нормы»). Waknuk, Strorm, David, Rosalind, Petra, Sophie, Axel, Michael, Anne, Katherine, Sally stay as
  in the book; Mrs Wender — Frau Wender; the Fringes — die Randgebiete; Sealand — Seeland; the Old People — die Alten;
  the Definitions — die Definitionen; a Deviation — eine Abweichung; the Spider-man — der Spinnenmann; great-horses —
  Großpferde; the meeting-house — das Versammlungshaus.
- **The Fall of the House of Usher** — «Der Untergang des Hauses Usher», as German editions have it; Roderick Usher,
  Madeline, the knight Ethelred stay; the tarn — der Teich; the vault — die Gruft.
- The player is «du»; a replay is «neue Partie», the main run «Hauptpartie».
