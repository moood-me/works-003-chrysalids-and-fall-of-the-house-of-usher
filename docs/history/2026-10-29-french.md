# 2026-10-29 — French (`fr`)

Why: Павел: ещё язык — французский.

- Both worlds and both games are in French too: `"fr"` in their `languages`; `lang/fr.json`, each scene's
  `scenes/<folder>/lang/fr.json`, the games' `bespoke/lang/fr/strings.json`.
- «The Chrysalids» — «Les Chrysalides»: David Strorm, Waknuk, les Franges, les Déviations, les Définitions, Blasphème,
  Offense, l’Image, Sealand. The narrator in the passé simple, spoken French in dialogue, «tu» in the interface. The
  hymn is data by language: `bespoke/lang/fr/hymn.json` — a French text of its own, the same notes and as many
  syllables a line as en/ru («Garde pur le sang du Seigneur…»). Signs drawn in the scenes are translated, as in Russian.
- «The Fall of the House of Usher» — «La Chute de la maison Usher», after Baudelaire: lines that quote Poe follow his
  translation (from memory — worth comparing with the edition); the characters say «vous» to each other, as in
  Baudelaire; lady Madeline.
- Translated from the English, the Russian read beside it. Checked with moood's `check` (its `fr` branch:
  `node packages/check/cli.js <repo>`) — «all right».
