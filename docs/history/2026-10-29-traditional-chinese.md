# Traditional Chinese (`zh-hant`) for works-003 (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: ещё язык — традиционный китайский». A third language beside English (the source) and Russian, for
readers in Taiwan and Hong Kong, in Taiwan's usage.

## What

- Both worlds and both games declare `"languages": ["en", "ru", "zh-hant"]`; every text has its `zh-hant`
  translation: the worlds' titles and descriptions, the scenes' titles and plates, the games' strings (lines, choices,
  the map, the author's panel), and the Chrysalids hymn (`games/chrysalids/bespoke/lang/zh-hant/hymn.json`: one
  syllable a character, the same notes, eight and ten syllables a line as in `en`).
- The world titles keep «v3» as the Russian does: 《蛹 v3》, 《亞瑟府的沒落 v3》.
- Names. **The Chrysalids** — 《蛹》 (the Chinese title of the novel, 人民文學出版社; no Taiwan edition found);
  the names in Taiwan-style transliteration: 大衛·史卓姆, 蘇菲, 羅莎琳, 佩特拉, 艾克索姑丈 (Axel is David's father's
  sister's husband), 約瑟, 艾蜜莉, 麥可, 安, 莎莉, 凱瑟琳, 馬克, 瑞秋, 艾倫·厄文, 溫德, 莫頓, 席巴; 瓦克納克
  (Waknuk), 西蘭 (Sealand), 拉布拉多, 邊緣地帶 (the Fringes), 古人 (the Old People), 檢查官, 蜘蛛人, 《定義》.
  **The Fall of the House of Usher** — 《亞瑟府的沒落》 (the Taiwan edition of 2023, 大牌出版, tr. 沈聿德, and the
  title Netflix uses in Taiwan); 羅德瑞克·亞瑟, 瑪德琳; 艾瑟瑞德 (the knight Ethelred); the seal's initial 「亞」.
