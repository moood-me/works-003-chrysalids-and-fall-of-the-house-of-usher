# 2026-10-29 — Brazilian Portuguese (`pt`)

Why: Павел: «Португальский (бразильский) еще запусти» — Brazilian Portuguese as a further language.

- Both worlds and both games are in Brazilian Portuguese too: `"pt"` in their `languages`; `lang/pt.json`, each
  scene's `scenes/<folder>/lang/pt.json`, the games' `bespoke/lang/pt/strings.json` (1026 texts in all).
- «The Chrysalids» — «As Crisálidas»: David Strorm, Waknuk, as Franjas (the Fringes), os Antigos (the Old People), as
  Definições, Desvio / desviante (Deviation, deviant), Blasfêmia, Ofensa, a Imagem, Zelândia (Sealand), o Homem-Aranha
  (the Spider-man), os grandes cavalos (great-horses). The narrator in plain past tense, spoken Brazilian in dialogue,
  «você» for the player. Signs drawn in the scenes translated in the old imperative of a sect («Honra a Imagem»,
  «Mantém pura a estirpe», «Guarda-te do Mutante!», «Bendita seja a Norma»); the Old People's plate «…ÇÃO».
- The hymn is data by language: `bespoke/lang/pt/hymn.json` — the same notes and as many sung syllables a line as
  en/ru (8, 8, 10, 8), vowels run together where Portuguese sings them so: «Mantém pura a estirpe do Senhor» /
  «Maldito quem não é à Sua Imagem».
- «The Fall of the House of Usher» — «A Queda da Casa de Usher»: the lines that quote Poe in an elevated, slightly old
  register (translated here, not taken from a Brazilian edition); the tarn — o lago; «você» between Roderick and the
  narrator, as the player is «você», with a formal «lhe» where it sounds right.
- Translated from the English; the French, Spanish and Russian looked at for the drawn signs and the hymn. Checked with moood's `check` (its `pt`
  branch): «all right — 1026 texts in 37 scopes».
