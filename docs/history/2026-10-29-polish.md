# 2026-10-29 — Polish (`pl`)

Why: Павел: «и польский» — Polish as a further language beside the nine.

- The two games and their worlds: `"pl"` in `languages`, every world's and scene's `lang/pl.json`, each game's
  `bespoke/lang/pl/strings.json` (1026 texts) and the hymn's `games/chrysalids/bespoke/lang/pl/hymn.json` — the same
  notes, the same syllables a line (8/8/10/8), «Niech czysty będzie Pański ród», the top note on a stressed syllable.
- «Poczwarki» (Wyndham, Królicki's translation): David Strorm, Rosalind, Sophie, Petra, Axel — the English spellings,
  as the edition keeps them; Obrzeża (the Fringes), Zelandia (Sealand), Dawni Ludzie, Definicje, Odchylenie, Pająk.
  «Zagłada domu Usherów» (Poe): Roderyk Usher, Madeline, Ethelred; the opening sentence after Leśmian's.
- Register: the player is «ty»; Roderyk and the narrator say «ty» to each other, David «pan» to the inspector.
- Seen, not changed: Usher's handwriting font (`games/fall-of-the-house-of-usher/bespoke/fonts.css`, Bad Script)
  has Latin and Cyrillic letters only — ą ę ł ń ś ź ż fall back to another font inside a word.

How it was checked: moood's `check` (its `pl` branch: `pl` in `i18n/languages.json`), `node packages/check/cli.js <repo> --write`
laid the files out, the translations were written into them, then `check` on the whole repository says «all right».
From the English source, the Russian read beside it as a second view of meaning and tone. Only translations and the
`languages` lists were added; no source text, code or doc was changed.
